Privacy Policy

CatScan - Breed Identifier

Last updated: February 19, 2026

Edge Analytics ("we", "our", or "us") operates the CatScan mobile application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our app.

Information We Collect

Photos

When you use the breed identification feature, your photo is sent to a third-party AI service (OpenAI) for analysis. Photos are processed in real-time and are not stored on our servers. OpenAI's data usage policy applies to the processing of these images.

Subscription Data

If you subscribe to CatScan Pro, your subscription is managed through Apple's App Store and RevenueCat. We receive an anonymous identifier to verify your subscription status. We do not receive your name, email, or payment details.

Scan History

Your scan history (breed results and photos) is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. This data never leaves your device unless you choose to share it.

Diagnostics

Apple may collect anonymized crash reports on our behalf. These contain no personally identifiable information.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or phone number
  • We do not collect location data
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites
  • We do not use advertising identifiers
  • We do not share any data with third parties for marketing purposes

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Breed Identification: Photos are sent to OpenAI's API solely to identify cat breeds
  • Subscription Management: To verify your access to premium features via RevenueCat

We do not use your data for any other purpose.

Third-Party Services

OpenAI

Used for AI-powered breed identification. Photos are sent to OpenAI's servers for processing and are not retained after analysis. See OpenAI's Privacy Policy.

RevenueCat

Used for subscription management. RevenueCat processes subscription-related data using an anonymous device identifier. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Data Storage and Security

All scan history and breed results are stored locally on your device. We do not operate any servers that store your personal data. We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties.

Data Retention

Photos sent for identification are processed in real-time and not retained. Your local scan history remains on your device until you delete it within the app or remove the app entirely.

Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over it:

  • Delete individual scans within the app at any time
  • Remove all data by deleting the app

No account or server-side data exists to request deletion of.

Children's Privacy

CatScan is rated for ages 4+ and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not require any personal information to function.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
