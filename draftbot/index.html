draftbot

AI-generated social content, pushed straight to drafts.

What it is

draftbot is an internal tool operated by Edge Analytics. It generates short-form social media content (text + images) using AI providers and pushes it to our own connected TikTok account as drafts, where a human reviews and publishes manually.

How it works

  • Picks a topic from a brand content pack
  • Generates a short script and caption with Anthropic Claude
  • Generates accompanying images with Google Imagen
  • Uploads the result to TikTok as a draft via the Content Posting API
  • A human reviews and publishes from the TikTok app

Scope

draftbot is a single-operator tool. It is not offered as a service to third parties. It only interacts with TikTok accounts that the operator owns and has explicitly authorized.
