Northline

A compass for iPhone

Features

A Needle You Can Trust

The needle is only drawn solid when the reading deserves it. Live accuracy is shown as ±N°, a calibration prompt appears exactly when it's needed, and if heading data stops, the needle hollows out — a frozen compass never looks like a working one.

True North, Really

True and magnetic north are one tap apart and the choice is remembered. Coordinates, altitude and declination (with its sign) stay on screen — the readouts other compasses removed.

Qibla, Free Forever

A great-circle bearing to the Kaaba on the same instrument face, always referenced to true north, with the distance to Mecca and alignment feedback. Never behind the paywall.

Six Instrument Faces

Classic is free; Ink, Brass, Contrast, Night and Lume are the Pro perk. Night keeps every colour red so it won't wreck your dark adaptation — a property enforced by a test, not by eye.

Common questions

The compass seems wrong. Hold the phone flat — tilt is the physical cause of most compass errors, and Northline shows a level bubble plus a warning when tilt passes 12°. If accuracy is poor, the app asks for a figure-8 calibration.

Qibla shows no direction. The Qibla bearing needs your location once. Allow location access when asked — it is used on the device only and never leaves it.

What does the ± number mean? It is the sensor's own reported heading accuracy: green within ±10°, amber to ±25°, red beyond. Northline shows it instead of pretending.

How do I restore my Pro purchase? Northline Pro is a one-time lifetime purchase tied to your Apple ID. Open Settings inside the app and tap Restore Purchases on any new device.
