Privacy Policy

Northline

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Edge Analytics ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Northline mobile application, a compass for iPhone. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our app.

The Short Version

  • Northline requires no account and works fully offline.
  • Your location is used on the device only — for the Qibla bearing, true north and the readouts. It is never stored by us and never leaves your iPhone.
  • The free tier shows ads served by Google AdMob. We do not use the App Tracking Transparency framework and do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • Buying Northline Pro removes ads; the purchase is handled by Apple and RevenueCat.
  • Northline contains no analytics SDK.

Location

Northline asks for location access to compute true north, the Qibla bearing, your coordinates, altitude and the local magnetic declination. All of it happens on your device. Your location is never stored by us, never sent to our servers (we run none), and never shared. Deny the permission and the compass still works on magnetic north.

Information Stored on Your Device

Saved waypoints, the north-reference choice, units, theme and language override are stored locally on your iPhone. We have no access to them. Deleting the app removes them.

Advertising (Free Tier)

The free version of Northline shows banner ads and an occasional full-screen ad served by Google AdMob. Every ad request is explicitly marked as non-personalized, and ad content is capped at a general-audiences rating. Northline does not use Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework and does not access the advertising identifier (IDFA), so ads are not based on tracking you across other companies' apps and websites.

To serve ads, measure their performance, and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • Device information (model, operating system, language)
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, taps)

Ad attribution uses Apple's privacy-preserving SKAdNetwork framework. For details on how Google processes this data, see Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from ads.

Purchasing Northline Pro removes all advertising, and with it all AdMob data processing.

Purchases

Northline Pro is a one-time purchase processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to validate purchases and manage your Pro entitlement. RevenueCat processes a pseudonymous app-user identifier and your purchase history for this purpose. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Analytics and Tracking

Northline contains no analytics SDK. We do not collect usage statistics, do not use advertising identifiers, and do not track you across other apps or websites.

Data Retention

Since all app data lives on your device, retention is under your control: deleting the app deletes the data. Purchase records are retained by Apple and RevenueCat as needed to keep your Pro entitlement working (including Restore Purchases on a new device).

Your Rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. GDPR in the EU), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. Because we hold no personal data about you ourselves, such requests usually concern Google (advertising) or RevenueCat (purchases) — but you can always contact us and we will help route your request.

Children's Privacy

Northline is a navigation tool suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Advertising in the free tier is non-personalized, capped at a general-audiences content rating, and served without cross-app tracking.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
