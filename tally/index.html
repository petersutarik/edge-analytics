Tally

A tally counter for iPhone

Features

Count Without Aiming

Tap anywhere on the screen to count — the whole surface is the button. Press and hold to take one back, undo walks back the last fifty changes, and reset asks first. A blocked tap at zero even feels different from a counted one.

Dhikr That Keeps Its Own Rounds

The tasbih presets count to 33 or 99, bank the round, and start again on their own — a full 99 is three rounds without ever touching a reset button. Preset names are localized in all eleven languages.

Counts From Your Home Screen

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets carry a + button that counts without opening the app. "Add to Counter" works from Shortcuts, Siri, and the Action button, and everything stays in sync.

One Counter for Everything

Laps, knitting rows, stock, attendance — each counter has its own step, target, colour, and tag, with a progress ring and a completion buzz. Export every counter and every recorded change as CSV. Tally Pro removes the banner ads and the counter limit with one purchase — no subscription.

Common questions

How do I subtract a count? Press and hold anywhere on the counting screen, or use the − button. Undo steps back through the last fifty changes and restores the exact prior value — including a reset.

My counter won't go below zero. That's deliberate for laps and rows, where a negative count is always a misfire. For stock adjustments, turn on "Allow negative values" in that counter's settings.

The widget shows an old number. The app, the widget, and Shortcuts all read and write the same storage, and iOS refreshes widgets on its own schedule — opening the app always brings everything back in sync.

How do I restore my Pro purchase? Tally Pro is a one-time lifetime purchase tied to your Apple ID. Open Settings inside the app and tap Restore Purchases on any new device.
