Privacy Policy

Fiber

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Edge Analytics ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Fiber mobile application, a fiber tracker for iPhone (called Fibre in the UK). This page explains what information the app handles and where it goes.

The Short Version

  • Fiber requires no account. We run no servers that receive your data.
  • Your food log, goals and answers stay on your iPhone.
  • The only thing that leaves your phone is a barcode number, sent to Open Food Facts when you scan packaged food.
  • Apple Health is optional and write-only: Fiber can save the fiber you log, and never reads your Health data.
  • Fiber contains no ads, no analytics SDK and no tracking.

Information Stored on Your Device

Fiber stores the following locally on your iPhone, in storage that only Fiber and its widgets can read:

  • Your food entries (food, amount, fiber, time) and your favourite foods
  • Your goal settings and the answers you give during setup (sex, age band and roughly how much fiber you eat now), used only to calculate your daily target and ramp-up plan
  • Gut check-ins (bloating and regularity), if you use them
  • Products you have scanned, cached so a second scan works offline
  • App preferences such as reminder times and haptics

We have no access to any of it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Barcode Lookups

When you scan or enter a barcode, Fiber sends that barcode number to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org), a free community food database, to look up the product's name and nutrition. The request contains the barcode and nothing about you: no account, name, food log or device identifier. Like any web server, Open Food Facts receives your IP address with the request. See the Open Food Facts privacy policy.

Searching the built-in food database (from USDA FoodData Central) happens entirely on your device.

Camera

Fiber uses the camera only to read barcodes. The picture is processed live on your device and is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

Apple Health

If you turn on Save to Apple Health, Fiber saves each entry to the Health app as a dietary fiber sample, and updates or removes it when you edit or delete the entry. Fiber only asks for permission to write dietary fiber and never reads any Health data. Data from Apple Health is not used for advertising, is not shared with anyone, and is not stored in iCloud by Fiber. You can turn this off at any time in Fiber's Settings or in the Health app.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. They are not sent through any server of ours.

Analytics and Tracking

Fiber contains no analytics SDK and no advertising. We do not collect usage statistics, do not use advertising identifiers, and do not track you across other apps or websites.

Purchases

Fiber currently has no in-app purchases. To recognise early users, the app may read Apple's record of when you first downloaded it. That check happens on your device through the App Store, and we never receive it.

Exporting and Deleting Your Data

Settings → Export CSV saves all your entries to a file you control. Deleting the app removes everything Fiber stored on your iPhone. Entries already saved to Apple Health stay there until you delete them in the Health app.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. GDPR in the EU and UK), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Because we hold no personal data about you, such requests are usually answered by the app itself (export or delete) — but you can always contact us and we will help.

Children's Privacy

Fiber is a general-audience nutrition app and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Medical Disclaimer

Fiber provides general nutrition information, not medical advice. If you have a digestive condition, talk to your doctor before changing your diet.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and update the "Last updated" date. If a future version of Fiber adds a feature that sends data off your device, this page will say so before that version is released.
