Fiber

A fiber tracker for iPhone

Features

Log in Seconds

Search nearly 900 foods offline, scan the barcode on packaged food, pick a real portion like "1 cup" or "1 slice", and re-log favourites with one tap.

Ramp Up Gently

Adding fiber too fast is the classic way to end up bloated. Fiber starts a little above what you eat now and raises your goal a few grams each week until you reach your target.

30 Plants a Week

Fiber counts the different plants you eat each week. Red lentils and lentil soup count as one plant, so the number means something.

Private by Design

No account, no ads, no analytics. Your log stays on your iPhone. Widgets show today's progress, and you can save each entry to Apple Health if you like.

Common questions

Where does my daily goal come from? From the US Dietary Reference Intakes for your sex and age (25–38 g), or the NHS 30 g if your iPhone's region is the UK. You can set your own in Settings → Daily goal.

Why is my goal lower than the recommendation? That's the ramp-up plan. It raises your goal a few grams each week so your gut can adjust. Turn it off or change the weekly step in Settings → Plan.

A barcode isn't found. Packaged food comes from Open Food Facts, a free community database. If a product is missing or has no fiber listed, Fiber offers Quick add with the name filled in.

How do I get my data out? Settings → Export CSV saves every entry to a spreadsheet file.

Fiber isn't showing up in Apple Health. Turn on Save to Apple Health in Fiber’s Settings, then check that Fiber is allowed to write Dietary Fiber in the Health app under Sharing → Apps → Fiber.

Fiber gives general nutrition information, not medical advice. If you have a digestive condition, talk to your doctor before changing your diet.
