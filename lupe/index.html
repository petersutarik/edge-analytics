Lupe

A magnifying glass for iPhone

Features

Real Magnification, Up to 20×

The telephoto or macro lens is chosen automatically, so Lupe magnifies more than the stock camera. Zoom by slider or pinch — the current factor is always on screen, and stabilisation keeps 15× readable hand-held.

Light That Doesn't Blind

The torch has a brightness slider, not just on/off — full brightness on a glossy label is a mirror. Tap to focus, lock the focus, and push it closer with a manual slider for curved bottles.

Six Reading Filters

Normal, grey, sharp, black-on-yellow, yellow-on-black and inverted — the classic low-vision pairings, applied to the live picture. Freeze in one tap; Save writes exactly the pixels on screen.

Made for Older Eyes

Big type, high contrast, 56pt round controls and VoiceOver labels on every one. Controls hide themselves so the whole screen is picture — nothing, including the ad, is ever on top of what you're reading.

Common questions

The picture is dark. Turn on the light with the torch button and slide its brightness up until the label reads without glare. The light switches off automatically when you leave the app.

How do I keep the image still? Tap the freeze button (the snowflake) — the frame stops instantly. Frozen frames keep their controls, and Save puts exactly what you see into Photos.

The text is sharp but hard to read. Cycle the filter button: black-on-yellow and yellow-on-black are made for tired and low-vision eyes.

How do I restore my Pro purchase? Lupe Pro is a one-time lifetime purchase tied to your Apple ID. Open Settings inside the app and tap Restore Purchases on any new device.
