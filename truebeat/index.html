Truebeat

A metronome and tuner for iPhone

Features

The Click That Doesn't Drift

Every beat is scheduled sample-accurately against the audio clock, so the tempo cannot drift over a long take. It keeps playing with the screen locked, and start/stop and ±5 BPM work from the lock screen.

A Real Metronome

30–260 BPM with a rotary dial, buttons, and tap tempo. Time signatures from 1/4 to 7/4 plus 3/8, 6/8, 9/8, and 12/8, subdivisions, and a per-beat accent pattern you can mute. A haptic-only mode lets you practice in silence.

Chromatic Tuner

Works for any instrument, with no picker to get wrong. A cents readout with a ±5¢ in-tune window and a pitch history strip — not just a green light. A4 is calibratable from 415 to 466 Hz.

Six Instrument Faces (Pro)

Classic amber, quiet Ink, lacquered Brass, high-visibility Contrast, stage-safe dim red, and luminous Lume. Pro also unlocks unlimited presets and setlists and every click voice — and removes all ads. One purchase, no subscription.

Common questions

The tuner doesn't react. The tuner needs microphone access — turn it on in iOS Settings → Truebeat. The metronome works without it.

How do I restore my Pro purchase? Truebeat Pro is a one-time lifetime purchase tied to your Apple ID. Open Settings inside the app and tap Restore Purchases on any new device.

Does the click keep playing when I lock the screen? Yes. The click continues with the screen locked, and you can start, stop, and nudge the tempo ±5 BPM straight from the lock screen.

My orchestra tunes to 442 Hz. Open the tuner and set the Reference A4 anywhere from 415 to 466 Hz — readings recalibrate instantly.
